Puebloans

Cultural, Economic

Starting Bias: Desert, cliffs

Unlocks: Aztec, Apache, Mexico, United States

UA:

People of the Cliffs

Rough terrain provides +1 culture. Doubled if rough desert.
Cliffs tiles provide +1 culture adjacencies to buildings.
UU:

Unique Civilian:

Hisatsimom

When founding a city, receive 5 culture for every desert within three times of the city center.
Unique Buildings:

Kiva:

Culture base building. +2 culture.
+1 Culture for adjacent quarters.
+2 culture if built on a tile containing/bordering a cliff.
Great House:

Food base building. +3 food.
+3 food if on a tile containing/bordering a cliff.
Unique District:

Pueblo:

Acts as a fortified district that must be conquered.
+5 gold if on a tile containing/bordering a cliff.
Policies:

Basketmaker Culture:

Tier 1

Unlock the “Chaco Road” tradition.
Buildings receive +1 gold if built on rough terrain.
Tier 2

Unlock the Kiva.
When a Hisatsimom founds a city, receive 5 gold for every rough tile within 3 tiles of the city center.
Cliff Dwellers:

Tier 1:

Unlock the Great House.
Unlock the Cliff Palace
Tier 2:

Unlock the “Sky City” Tradition
Traditions

Chaco Road:

Roads on desert tiles yield +2 culture.
Sky Cities:

Units receive a defensive bonus on rough tiles and tiles with cliffs.
Adobe:

Gold buildings receive +1 production adjacency with resources.
Production buildings receive +1 gold adjacency with resources.
Associated Wonder

Cliff Palace:

Must be built on a desert tile adjacent or on a cliff.
All desert tiles in this settlement provide +2 culture. Counts as a fortified district.
Goal:

A civ that thrives in rocky deserts, turning rough terrain and cliffs into food and culture.
